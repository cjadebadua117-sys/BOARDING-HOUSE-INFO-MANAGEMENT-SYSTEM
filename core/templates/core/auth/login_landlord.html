Get your boarding house listed

One visit to the campus housing office is the whole process.

  1. Visit the housing office. Drop by the DMMMSU–NLUC campus housing office with a valid ID.
  2. Bring proof of ownership. A title, a lease, or a letter from the owner of the boarding house.
  3. Collect your credentials. Staff create the account and hand you the username and a first password.
  4. Sign in and publish. Add your boarding houses and rooms, then answer the student inquiries that follow.
